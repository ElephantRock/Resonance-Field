# Experiment 004 — freshness-aware reputation

## Question

Can freshness-aware reputation preserve useful organizational memory while reducing stale-incumbent retention under repeated context shifts?

## Why this follows Experiment 003

Experiment 003 showed that raw persistent domain reputation changes collective organization, particularly when substrate traces decay quickly, but does not improve overall delegation success and increases early post-shift incumbent retention.

Experiment 004 therefore changes only the **active influence** of old reputation evidence. The underlying evidence ledger remains cumulative and auditable.

## Initial arms

- no reputation;
- raw persistent domain reputation;
- domain reputation with freshness half-lives of 6, 12, 24, and 48 cycles.

All arms see the same deterministic tasks, candidate sets, market noise, practice dynamics, and repeated domain-to-skill regime shifts.

## Decision rule

A candidate may determine Experiment 005 only if it remains within the campaign's hard success and incumbency tolerances relative to the no-reputation control. Among feasible candidates, the constrained utility selects the next center policy.

The measured dominant failure mode of that selected policy determines which policy dimension Experiment 005 tests. No later experiment question is fixed by this document.
