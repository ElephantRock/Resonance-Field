# Coordination Topology Campaign — Experiments 087–092

## Hypothesis

Resonance Field lock-in is generated primarily by the coordination mechanism: repeated task–agent opportunity structure can reproduce incumbent control even when identity, reputation, private capability, and public knowledge are not the intervention target.

The campaign therefore holds agents immortal and reputation-neutral and intervenes only on the candidate-opportunity graph. The causal object is the directed task-domain → agent exposure topology observed before each award.

## Sequence

- **087 — topology screen:** baseline random opportunity graph vs global exposure balancing, domain-edge balancing, incumbent-edge cooldown, and a hybrid intervention.
- **088 — decomposition and temporal precedence:** remove winner cooldown or domain balancing from the selected mechanism and verify that opportunity topology changes before allocation outcomes.
- **089 — bounded response:** vary the structured-routing fraction around the selected intervention and require a non-fragile quality/plasticity region.
- **090 — rapid shift + reversal:** freeze the intervention under faster remapping and include a within-run restoration of the baseline routing rule to test whether lock-in is recreated without restoring old agent state.
- **091 — independent replication:** frozen mechanism on independent seeds.
- **092 — unseen holdout:** unseen seeds/remap schedule plus a predeclared restoration prediction.

## Discovery gate

Relative to the baseline coordination rule, a candidate must preserve all market/economic/provenance invariants, lose no more than 1.5 percentage points of task success, reduce logical early-incumbent share by at least 2 percentage points, preserve late public knowledge within 10 percentage points, require zero identity turnover, and materially reduce pre-award incumbent opportunity exposure.

A validated discovery additionally requires bounded-response robustness, rapid-shift survival, independent replication, unseen holdout validation, and topology-before-allocation temporal ordering.

Production remains reputation-neutral regardless of outcome.
