# Matching Objective Campaign 093–098

This campaign tests whether Resonance Field's persistent logical allocation pattern is generated by the sealed-bid **assignment objective** rather than by agent identity, reputation, private capability persistence, or candidate-opportunity topology.

The experiment keeps production behavior unchanged. Inside each isolated cell, it temporarily replaces only the deterministic bid-score function used by the existing market award path, then restores the production scorer immediately after the cell.

## Sequence

- **093:** screen baseline, confidence-light, price/speed-only, capped-confidence, and geometric-balance objectives.
- **094:** exact sealed-bid replay under baseline and selected objectives; require objective replay to reproduce the actual award exactly.
- **095:** bounded intervention-strength response.
- **096:** faster remapping plus within-run restoration of the baseline objective without agent-state reset.
- **097:** independent seeds with the objective frozen.
- **098:** unseen seeds/remap schedule and a frozen restoration/re-lock prediction.

## Direct causal evidence

Every task persists an append-only `matching_objective_observations` row per sealed bid containing the production score, experimental objective score, actual selection, and both counterfactual selections. Candidate sets are verified against the baseline deterministic candidate generator.

A candidate must preserve all invariants, lose no more than 1.5 percentage points of task success, reduce logical early-incumbent share by at least 2 percentage points, preserve late public knowledge within 10 percentage points, change a material share of winners on exact-bid replay, and show a same-bid logical-plasticity improvement.

Production remains reputation-neutral and uses the original market objective regardless of campaign outcome.
