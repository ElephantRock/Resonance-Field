# Delayed-Onset Phase Observability — Experiments 111–116

Canonical preregistration: GitHub issue #44.

This campaign tests whether the sign of the already-frozen aligned endogenous-demand feedback mechanism (λ=0.5) is prospectively predictable from organizational state **after an exogenous burn-in long enough to form two complete regimes**.

The standard treatment and control are identical through cycle 35. At cycle 36, treatment activates aligned feedback while control remains exogenous. The five state observables are measured only from the pre-activation history. Discovery may freeze at most one scalar threshold. That threshold must then transfer without refitting to independent seeds, a three-regime activation time, and a different regime-period holdout.

No additional feature, λ value, activation time, seed, composite model, or post-treatment predictor may be introduced after Experiment 112 begins.

The campaign is a prerequisite to the external-boundary architecture in issue #42; it is not itself an external-boundary test. Production behavior remains unchanged and reputation-neutral.
